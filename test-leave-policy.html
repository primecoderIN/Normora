Employee Leave Policy

Normora Test Company | Policy owner: People Operations | Effective date: 1 January 2026 | Version 1.0

Summary: Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave should be requested in advance and approved by the employee's manager.

1. Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 20 working days of paid annual leave for each complete calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted working hours. Employees joining during the year receive a pro-rated allowance based on their start date.

Annual leave is intended to support rest and personal commitments. Employees should plan leave throughout the year and avoid allowing a large balance to build up without a business reason.

2. Request and Approval Process

Employees must submit a leave request through the company leave system at least 10 working days before the requested start date. The manager reviews team coverage, business deadlines, and overlapping requests before approving or declining the request.

Leave is not approved until the request is confirmed in the leave system. Employees should not make non-refundable travel arrangements until approval has been received.

3. Carry-Over

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they will expire, unless People Operations has approved an exception in writing.

4. Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work because of illness must notify their manager before their normal start time on the first day of absence. A medical certificate may be requested for absences lasting more than 3 consecutive working days.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and must not be recorded as annual leave. Managers should contact People Operations if an absence becomes long-term or if additional workplace support may be required.

5. Public Holidays and Non-Working Days

Public holidays and scheduled non-working days are not deducted from an employee's annual leave balance. When annual leave spans a public holiday, only the employee's normal working days are counted.

6. Questions and Policy Exceptions

Questions about leave balances, eligibility, or exceptional circumstances should be sent to People Operations. Any exception to this policy must be approved in writing by the Head of People Operations before the leave is taken.
